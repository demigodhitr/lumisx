About

{{app_name}} is a next-gen peer-to-peer investment platform that connects traders and investors through secure, escrow-backed transactions. We empower individuals to grow wealth with transparency, safety, and confidence.

{{app_name}} uses a proxy trading system backed by a secure escrow model. When you start a trade, your capital is temporarily held in a secure, isolated pool — not directly handed to the trader. Think of it like handing your sealed envelope of cash to a trusted vault while the trader gets access only to a dashboard showing how much they're allowed to trade with — not where it's stored.

They can influence how your capital is allocated for market actions, but they cannot move, withdraw, or access your funds. This setup allows professional traders to generate returns on your behalf without ever touching your actual wallet or bank.
  • 🔐 Security by design: Traders never access your personal account, banking details, or wallet. They cannot view your sensitive data, perform withdrawals, or initiate any financial transactions.
You remain in full control. Only you can withdraw your profits or capital at any time, based on your trading plan's terms. This ensures maximum privacy, security, and ownership at every point in your journey.

{{app_name}} virtual cards are used as a bridge between your funds and the trading system. These cards are required to:
  • Validate your identity
  • Pull funds from the central ledger pool — the escrow where your returns accumulate
  • Withdraw to external exchanges
Because {{app_name}} virtual Cards are tied to how the system manages secure fund flows like identity verification, they must be active prior to processing outgoing transactions.
  • 🚫 Accrued profits cannot be used to request for the card — since the card itself is the channel used to release those profits.

  • Imagine booking a ride on Uber. The driver (trader) takes you to your destination (profits), but they don’t get access to your bank account. Uber (Lumis X) handles all the logistics, tracks the route, protects your info, and only releases the payment when the ride is done and you're happy.
